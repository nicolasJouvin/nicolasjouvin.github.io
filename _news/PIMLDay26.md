We planned a nice program & list of speakers covering a wide range or area from statistical learning to modeling and numerical analysis ! The event will also have a poster session.

It will take place in the beautiful AgroParisTech campus, in the heart of our beloved *Plateau de Saclay* Registration is free but mandatory. All the info are on the conference website (french & english version)
